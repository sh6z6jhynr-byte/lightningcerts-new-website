QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Which certificates are supported?

The current app includes EIC, Minor Works, EICR, Solar PV and EV Charging workflows.

Can I keep customer and property history?

Lightning Certs is designed to keep customers, properties and certificates together for repeat visits.

Can I preview the finished certificate?

Yes. A completed certificate can be previewed as a PDF before you share it.

Is the app designed for iPhone?

Yes. The current interface is built with a focused iPhone workflow, with an iPad experience alongside it.
